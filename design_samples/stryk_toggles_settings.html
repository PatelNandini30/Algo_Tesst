The pairing that works

Switch + caption · grouped in an Advanced accordion

Keep the familiar switch (with helper text), but stop them flooding the config: fold the six advanced toggles into a collapsible "Advanced rules & adjustments" group, expanded only when needed.

Why this pairing
→ Switch + caption stays familiar and scannable — no relearning.
→ Advanced accordion cuts the default scroll by ~60%, surfacing only Index/Expiry/Dates first.
→ A "2 on" badge shows active rules without expanding.
→ Tri-state segments stay for non-binary settings (re-entry mode, underlying).
If you want denser instead
Pair checkbox-chips (style 03) with a chip toolbar (placement 05) — every toggle in one strip above the legs. Best for power users on wide screens.
Effort
Low — wrap existing toggles in a collapsible group. Pure presentation; no behavior change.
