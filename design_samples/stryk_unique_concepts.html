Field Notes · 02 / 07
Where they all look the same — and the gap

The convergence problem

Referenced AlgoTest · Stockmock · QuantMan · Streak · Tradetron · FX Replay · QuantJourney · BuildAlpha · SpotGamma / Quant Data.

What everyone already ships

Equity curve · drawdown chart · KPI tiles · trade-by-trade table · payoff diagram (AlgoTest/Stockmock) · chart tabs. Necessary — but identical across every tool. A user can't tell one screenshot from another.

The unclaimed ground

Probabilistic honesty (Monte Carlo cones), strategy fingerprinting (radar), time-as-canvas (P&L calendar heatmaps), and data storytelling (annotated, narrated results). Proven in quant research, rare in retail backtesters.

Three convictions for STRYK

Show the distribution, not the anecdote. One equity curve is a single lucky path. A Monte-Carlo cone earns trust by showing the range.

Give every strategy a face. A radar fingerprint makes two strategies instantly comparable — and memorable.

Narrate the result. "Worst day −2.1% on 12 Mar; recovered in 8 sessions." Storytelling beats a wall of numbers.
